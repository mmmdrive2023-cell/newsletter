Powering Reef Island’s Future

New Electrical Substation

Building a stronger, more reliable energy network for today and tomorrow.

Article 01 · New Electrical Substation

A stronger foundation for the island’s next chapter.

The new electrical substation is a long-term resilience investment— strengthening Reef Island’s power network today while preparing the island for future homes, amenities, and public-realm improvements.

The project introduces essential distribution infrastructure designed to improve reliability, increase capacity, and support the island’s continued growth. With modern equipment and upgraded protection systems, the new substation will help deliver a more stable and efficient power supply for residents, businesses, and future development phases.

Beyond technical performance, the initiative reflects a broader commitment to high-quality infrastructure that supports everyday life. Dependable utility services are fundamental to resident comfort, operational continuity, and long-term investment across the island.

Planning and coordination are progressing through key pre-delivery stages, with a focus on safety, efficiency, and smooth integration into the wider network. Future updates will continue to share milestone progress, anticipated works, and practical community information as the project advances.

Resident convenience remains central throughout the delivery approach. Access planning, logistics activity, service-related notices, and construction coordination will be communicated in advance to ensure transparency and carefully managed impact.

All Day Market

A convenient island destination for everyday essentials, fresh selections, and local favorites.

Article 02 · All Day Market

Everyday essentials, available around the clock.

All Day Market is now open 24 hours a day, giving Reef Island residents convenient access to groceries, fresh produce, bakery items, beverages, household essentials, and everyday necessities whenever they are needed.

Whether it is an early start, a late return home, or an unexpected last-minute need, the market offers residents the flexibility to shop at a time that suits their schedule. Its round-the-clock operation brings greater convenience directly into the community.

The retail selection brings together fresh produce, pantry staples, dairy products, bakery items, beverages, household supplies, and quick everyday options in one clean and welcoming environment. The aim is to make essential shopping simpler, faster, and closer to home.

The market has been planned as more than a retail outlet. Clear circulation, convenient access, practical servicing, and a comfortable customer environment help it feel like a natural part of Reef Island’s residential community.

By bringing useful products and extended operating hours closer to residents, All Day Market supports a more convenient lifestyle and gives the community more time for the people and activities that matter most.

Article 03 · Community Standards

Respect. Responsibility. Together.

Reef Island’s community standards help protect the safety, appearance, and quality of life enjoyed by residents, visitors, and partners across the island.

A well-managed community depends on simple, consistent habits. Responsible parking, proper waste disposal, considerate noise levels, and respect for landscaped and public areas all contribute to a cleaner and more comfortable environment.

These standards are designed to support everyday living rather than complicate it. Clear guidance helps residents understand what is expected, while regular monitoring and community support help maintain safe, orderly, and attractive surroundings.

Cooperation remains the most important part of successful community management. Small actions—parking correctly, keeping pathways clear, supervising pets, and being mindful of neighbours—make a visible difference.

Community notices and practical reminders will continue to be shared when needed. Residents requiring guidance or support can contact the Facilities and Community Management team.

Planning Today, Paving Tomorrow

Road Rehabilitation

Upgrading island roads for a smoother, safer drive and a more polished arrival.

Article 04 · Road Rehabilitation

Smoother roads. Stronger connections.

Reef Island’s road rehabilitation programme is improving safety, comfort, and long-term durability across key routes throughout the community.

The works include asphalt resurfacing, base strengthening, improved drainage, and refreshed road markings. Each stage is carefully coordinated to improve performance while maintaining access for residents, visitors, and service vehicles.

High-quality materials and professional site supervision will help extend the life of the road network, reduce maintenance needs, and improve everyday driving conditions across the island.

Work is being delivered in carefully planned phases to keep disruption to a minimum. Temporary traffic arrangements, access changes, and active work zones will be communicated through community notices where required.

Once complete, residents can expect smoother surfaces, clearer road guidance, better traffic flow, and a more polished arrival experience throughout Reef Island.

Lighting the Way Forward

LED Enhancement

More efficient lighting for brighter streets, safer paths, and a better evening experience.

Article 05 · LED Enhancement

Lighting up our community for a brighter tomorrow.

Reef Island’s LED Street Lighting Enhancement Programme is improving visibility, safety, energy efficiency, and the quality of the island’s public spaces after dark.

The programme replaces older street-lighting equipment with modern, energy-efficient LED technology. The new fixtures provide clearer, more consistent illumination while reducing electricity consumption and long-term maintenance requirements.

Improved lighting supports safer movement for pedestrians, cyclists, motorists, and evening visitors. Roads, walkways, landscaped areas, and shared community spaces will benefit from enhanced visibility and a more polished nighttime environment.

Installation is being delivered in phases to maintain continuity and minimize disruption. Work includes fixture replacement, electrical testing, alignment, and final illumination checks to ensure each area performs safely and consistently.

The programme supports Reef Island’s wider sustainability goals by lowering energy demand, reducing emissions, and extending the service life of the lighting network.
